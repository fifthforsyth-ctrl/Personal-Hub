Wake Watch texts

A personal accountability program run by Aaron Forsyth.

What it is

Aaron asked a few friends to hold him accountable for getting up at 4:30 each morning. His personal habit app sends each of them one automated text a day saying whether he got up on time. There is no marketing, no promotions, and nothing is sold. Recipients are never charged by us.

How people opt in

  1. Aaron asks a friend in person or by personal text whether they want to be one of his accountability partners and get one text each morning. Only people who say yes are added.
  2. Aaron enters their name and mobile number into his own app. Nobody else can add numbers, and there is no public sign-up form.
  3. The first message they receive confirms the opt-in and explains how to stop:
Aaron added you to his wake-up watch. Each morning around 4:35 you'll get one text: whether he's up fighting dragons, or when the sleep dragons won. Reply STOP to stop.

The verbal consent script

Aaron asks each friend in person or on a call, using these words:

"I'm working on getting up at 4:30. Would you be willing to be one of my accountability partners? My habit app would send you an automated text once a day, around 4:35 in the morning, saying whether I got up on time or what time I woke up. That's at most one text a day, about 30 a month. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply HELP for help, or STOP at any time and the texts stop. The terms and privacy policy are at forsyth-hub.vercel.app/sms-opt-in.html. Is that a yes or a no?"

Only a clear "yes" adds someone. Their number is then entered by Aaron, and the first text (above) confirms the enrollment.

What the messages say

Aaron is successfully up and fighting dragons
The sleep dragons have defeated Aaron--he woke up at 5:23 am

At most one message per day, sent between 4:35 am and 9:00 am Mountain Time.

Stopping and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages, and START to resume. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

Privacy policy

The only information kept is your name and mobile number, used only to send these texts. It is never shared, sold, or used for marketing, and no mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party. Your number is deleted when you stop or Aaron removes you.
